# Small Business Program

## Program overview

The Small Business Program supports early-stage and growing companies by offering discounted access to our software and services, scaled according to their financial capacity.

## Discount levels

| Financial Range (USD) | Discount |
|:---------------------:|:--------:|
|        0 - 1M         |   100%   |
|        1 – 2M         |    90%   |
|        2 – 3M         |    80%   |
|        3 – 4M         |    60%   |
|        4 – 5M         |    20%   |

## Program benefits

- Full access at no cost for organizations with financials under USD 1M.
- Scaled discounts for organizations with financials up to USD 5M.

## Definitions

- **Financials** is defined as the larger of the organization’s income, expenses, or assets in USD.
- **Organization** is defined as the top-most Entity which, directly or indirectly, controls all other Entities that will use the software.
- **Control** is defined as ownership of more than 50% of the voting power of an Entity.
- **Entity** is defined as an individual, body corporate, partnership, government or state or division thereof, government or state agency, or such other entity.

## FAQ

### Who is eligible?

All organizations that can provide proof of financials under USD 5M.

### Our consulting agency qualifies, but would this cover our clients?

If you own the intellectual property (IP), you're considered an independent software vendor (ISV) and, if eligible, the program would cover all your deployments.
If you don't own the IP and do custom development for each client, then the client organization must be eligible.

### How do I apply for the Small Business Program?

Contact us.

### Is support included?

No, the Small Business Program does not include support beyond the discussion forum.
You can buy support separately if you need it, but the discount does not apply to it.
